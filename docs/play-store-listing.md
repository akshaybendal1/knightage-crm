## Full description (max 4000)

```full
Knightage CRM is a sales CRM for teams that sell on the move. Manage every lead through a pipeline you define, never miss a follow-up, send branded quotations and invoices, and see where your field reps have actually been, all from your phone.

Sign in with your work email or your Google account. Team members join by invitation from their organization's admin.

PIPELINE AND LEADS
• Build your own pipeline stages, with the Won and Lost flags you choose
• Drag leads from stage to stage on the Timeline board
• One page per lead: activity timeline, tasks, meetings, products and documents in a single scroll
• Search and filter by stage, product or owner
• Import leads in bulk from a CSV file, and export any view to Excel
• Leads that sit in a stage for 14+ days are flagged on your dashboard automatically

TASKS AND FOLLOW-UPS
• Tasks with an owner and a due date, plus a calendar view of what's due
• A daily reminder by email and push notification covering what's due today, due tomorrow or overdue. Tap a notification to open the lead
• Message a lead on WhatsApp from a saved template, with their name filled in
• Work through a batch of leads one WhatsApp message at a time with the bulk queue

QUOTATIONS AND INVOICES
• Keep a product catalog with categories, and attach products to a lead with quantities
• Move each product through its own stages, separate from the lead
• Generate a PDF quotation or invoice with your logo, itemized products, discount and tax
• Email it to the lead and track it from Draft to Sent to Paid

FIELD SALES
• Log a customer visit with details and products. Each visit is saved with the GPS location of where you were
• Every visit becomes a lead in your normal pipeline, so there is nothing to reconcile later
• Admins see a coverage map of each rep's visits over a day, week or month
• Compare reps by visit count or value

REPORTS
• Eight built-in reports: lead volume, won and lost trends, sources, team performance, task completion and more
• Build your own reports and view them as a table, bar, line, pie or pivot
• Every dashboard figure links to the leads behind it

TEAM AND CONTROL
• Invite teammates as Admin, Member or Sales. Each role sees only what it needs, enforced on the server
• Your logo appears on every quotation and invoice
• See live usage against your plan's limits

YOUR DATA
Your data belongs to your organization. You can delete your account from Profile inside the app, and the privacy policy explains what is kept and what is removed.

Knightage CRM needs an organization account on a Knightage CRM plan. Questions? Write to contact@knightagetech.com.
```

## Before you publish

- **Google sign-in:** the description says "or your Google account". Keep that only after a real Google account has signed in on a phone. Otherwise cut those four words.
- **Google Calendar:** deliberately not mentioned. The client has `googleCalendarEnabled = false`, so the feature isn't live.
- **Pricing:** deliberately not mentioned. The only plans are paid (Basic and Pro), so don't call the app free, and Play can reject store-listing claims that don't match what a user finds.
- **Review access:** the Play review team needs a working login (Play Console > App content > App access). Create a throwaway organization for it. No password belongs in this file.
- **Screenshots:** the listing text promises the pipeline board, a lead page, a quotation PDF, reports and the visit log, so the screenshots should show those.
