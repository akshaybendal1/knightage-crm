KnightageCRM

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

This policy explains what information Knightage Tech ("Knightage", "we", "us") collects through the Knightage CRM web application and Android app (together, the "Service"), how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to everyone who uses the Service, whether through a web browser or the Android app.

1. Information we collect

Account information. When your organization creates an account or invites you as a user, we store your name, email address, phone number (if provided), role, and a securely hashed password. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from Google, and a Google account identifier used to link future sign-ins.

Business data you or your organization enter. The Service is a CRM: the people in your organization who use it enter leads, contacts, notes, tasks, meetings, documents, message templates, and related sales information about your organization's own customers and prospects. This information belongs to your organization; Knightage stores and processes it only to provide the Service. See "How we handle data belonging to your organization" below.

Location data. If your account has the Field Sales role and you use the "Add Entry" feature to log a lead while out in the field, the app asks your browser for your device's current GPS coordinates and attaches them to that lead record. This only happens when you actively use that feature — the Android app has no background or always-on location access, and no location permission is requested at all unless you use this specific feature.

Usage and log data. Like most web services, our servers automatically log standard technical information for security and troubleshooting purposes — IP address, browser or app version, device type, and timestamps of requests. We do not use this to build an advertising profile of you.

2. What we don't do

  • We don't include any third-party advertising or analytics SDKs (e.g. Google Analytics, Meta/Facebook SDK, ad networks) in the Android app or web client.
  • We don't sell your personal information, or your organization's business data, to anyone.
  • We don't use your data to serve you ads.

3. How we use information

We use the information above to: operate and maintain the Service; authenticate you and keep your account secure; provide customer support when you contact us; monitor for abuse, fraud, or technical problems; and improve the Service over time.

4. How we handle data belonging to your organization

Leads, contacts, and other business records entered into the CRM belong to the organization that entered them. Knightage acts as a service provider processing that data on the organization's behalf, solely to operate the Service — we do not use it for our own purposes, and we do not share it with other organizations using Knightage CRM. If you are the end customer or lead of a business using Knightage CRM (rather than someone with a login to the Service) and have questions about your data, please contact that business directly, as they control what's entered about you.

5. How we share information

We share information only with: infrastructure and hosting providers who run the servers the Service runs on, under confidentiality obligations; Google, when you choose to sign in with Google; and law enforcement or regulators, if we're required to by law. We do not otherwise sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties.

6. Data storage and security

Data is transmitted between your device and our servers over HTTPS/TLS. Access to production systems is restricted to authorized Knightage personnel. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable, industry-standard measures to protect your information.

7. Data retention

We retain account and business data for as long as your organization's account remains active, plus a reasonable period afterward for backups and legal/accounting purposes, after which it is deleted or anonymized. When you delete your own account (see section 8), your personal details are removed immediately; records that belong to your organization stay with it, shown as created by a "Deleted user".

8. Your rights and choices

You can review or correct your own account information from within the app, or ask us to do so.

Deleting your account. You can delete your account yourself at any time from My Profile → Delete account in the web app or the Android app. This immediately removes your name, email address, phone number, password, Google account link, and any Google Calendar connection, and signs you out. If you can't sign in, or you are the organization's owner (who can't use the in-app button because an organization must keep an owner), you can ask us to delete your account — or the whole organization and its data — by following the steps on our Delete your account page. We complete such requests within 30 days. If you're located in India, this is consistent with the rights available to you under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

9. Children's privacy

The Service is a business tool intended for use by working adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the Service after a change means you accept the updated policy.
