Delete your account

Last updated: 3 October 2026

This page explains how to delete your Knightage CRM account and what happens to your data. It applies to the web app and the Android app (com.knightage.crm).

Delete it yourself (immediate)

  1. Sign in to Knightage CRM on the web or in the Android app.
  2. Open My Profile from the menu at the top of the screen.
  3. Scroll to Delete account, type your account email to confirm (and your password, if you have one), then choose Delete my account.

Your account is removed straight away and you are signed out.

Can't sign in, or you own the organization?

Email us at contact@knightagetech.com from the email address on the account, with the subject Delete my Knightage CRM account. We will confirm the request came from you and complete it within 30 days.

The organization's owner can't use the in-app button, because every organization must keep an owner. If you're the owner, tell us whether you want only your own account removed (after another person takes over) or the entire organization and all of its data deleted.

What is deleted

  • Your name, email address, phone number, and password.
  • The link to your Google account, and any Google Calendar connection (including its stored access token).
  • Your ability to sign in. The email address becomes available again if you ever want to sign up anew.

What is kept

  • Records that belong to your organization — leads, contacts, notes, tasks, meetings, documents and their history — stay with the organization, since they are its business records. Anything you created or were assigned will show a Deleted user instead of your name, with none of your personal details attached.
  • Routine security logs and backups are kept for a limited period and then deleted or overwritten.
  • Anything we are legally required to retain.

If an entire organization is deleted at the owner's request, its business records are deleted as well, apart from anything we are legally required to keep.
