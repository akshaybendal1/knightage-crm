PIPELINE & LEADS

Every lead, tracked your way

A pipeline that matches your sales process, not the other way around — and one page per lead with everything that's ever happened on it.

Stages you define

Custom pipeline stages per organization — name, order, and Won/Lost flags you set. Create a new stage inline while adding a lead, no separate setup screen needed.

A board you can drag

The Timeline board shows every stage as a column — drag a lead (or a product line) straight from one stage to the next instead of hunting for a dropdown.

One page per lead

Activity timeline, tasks, meetings, products, and documents all live on the same Lead Detail page — notes, stage changes, and every document sent, in one scroll.

Find anything fast

Filter by stage, product, or owner, or just search. Bulk-import leads from a CSV template with per-row error reporting, and export any filtered view to Excel.

Built for a phone screen

Leads get a real mobile card layout on small screens — name and stage up top, quick-add chips for a meeting, note, task, or product, not a squeezed table.

Nothing goes stale

A lead sitting 14+ days with no stage change is flagged "stuck" automatically on your dashboard — before it quietly falls off your radar.

Won deals become customers

Move a lead to a Won stage and it becomes a customer. Older won leads can be converted in one go, or picked one by one, from the Customers screen.

Customers shared with Inventory

With Knightage Inventory, new customers are sent there for their first sales order, and customers added in Inventory show up here — matched so nobody is entered twice.

TASKS, CALENDAR & FOLLOW-UP

Never miss the next step

Tasks, a real calendar, and a daily reminder digest so a follow-up never just falls through.

Tasks with an owner

Every task has a due date and an assignee. Mark it complete in one click, and see everyone's overdue tasks from a single admin view.

A real calendar, not a list

A month-grid calendar of your tasks with color-coded overdue/due-today/upcoming/completed chips, plus a My/Team scope toggle for admins.

A daily reminder digest

Every user gets one email (and an Android push notification) each day summarizing what's due tomorrow, today, or overdue — deep-linking straight to the lead.

Message a lead on WhatsApp

Open a pre-filled WhatsApp message for any lead in one click, built from a reusable template with their name auto-filled in.

Bulk WhatsApp outreach

Select a batch of leads, pick a template, and work through a queue of ready-to-send WhatsApp links — one per lead, placeholders already resolved.

Templates your whole team shares

Write a message template once, reuse it everywhere — the composer, the bulk sender, and the dashboard's reminder shortcuts all pull from the same library.

PRODUCTS, QUOTATIONS & INVOICES

From line item to paid invoice

A product catalog leads can pull from, and real branded PDFs when it's time to bill.

A catalog with categories

Products with pricing and a managed category list. Attach any number of products to a lead, each with its own quantity — the lead's total is calculated automatically.

Each product, its own pipeline

Product Stages move independently of the parent lead's stage — a multi-product deal can have one line item Won while another is still in negotiation.

Branded quotations & invoices

Generate a real PDF straight from a lead's product lines — your logo, itemized products, discount and tax — no separate invoicing tool required.

Email it, then track it

Send the PDF straight to the lead's inbox and follow its status from Draft to Sent to Paid, with every step logged on the lead's own timeline.

REPORTS & INSIGHTS

The numbers, your way or ours

Eight built-in reports for the questions every sales team asks, plus a builder for the ones only you'd think to ask.

Build your own report

Pick dimensions — stage, owner, product, category, source, date — and a metric, then view it as a table, bar, line, pie, or pivot. Save it and re-run it anytime.

Eight reports, ready to go

Lead volume and won/lost trends, source breakdown, team performance, task completion, and leads by product, category, or owner — every one exportable to Excel.

A dashboard that leads somewhere

Every dashboard stat is a link — click a pipeline stage's value to see exactly which leads make it up, not just a number.

FIELD SALES & COVERAGE

Know where your team has actually been

A GPS-verified visit log for reps on the ground, and a map that shows their real coverage.

A visit log that can't be faked

Field reps log a doorstep visit with customer details and products — the form won't submit without a real GPS location attached.

Every visit, on a map

An admin-only coverage map plots each rep's visit pins over a day, week, or month, with a computed coverage area in km² — not just a list of addresses.

Compare reps at a glance

A rep-by-rep bar chart of visit count or value, so you know who's covering ground and who's covering revenue.

Same pipeline, no extra work

Every field visit becomes a regular lead in your normal pipeline automatically — nothing lives in a separate system that needs reconciling later.

TEAM, BRANDING & PLANS

Set it up the way your business runs

Invite your team, make it look like yours, and grow into the plan you need.

Roles that make sense

Invite teammates as Admin, Member, or Sales — each sees only what their role needs, enforced on the server, not just hidden in the UI.

Your logo, on every document

Upload your logo once and it appears on every quotation and invoice you generate from that point on.

See your plan usage live

Live usage bars for records and seats against your plan's limits, with a one-click request to your admin when it's time to upgrade.
